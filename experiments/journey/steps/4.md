# Step 4: no prereleases through a range

Keep everything from before. A version with a prerelease tag, such as `2.0.0-beta.1`, must never be chosen through a range, even when the range itself names a prerelease. A pin may still choose a prerelease version.

Example input:

    {"requires": {"next": "^14.0.0-rc.1", "vite": "^5.0.0"}, "available": {"next": ["14.0.0-rc.1", "14.0.0-rc.2"], "vite": ["5.0.0", "5.1.0-beta.0"]}}

Example output:

    {"resolved": {"vite": "5.0.0"}, "unresolved": ["next"], "suggestions": {}}
