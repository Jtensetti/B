# Step 4: bulky parcels

Keep everything from before. A catalog entry may now also have `"volume_l"`, the volume of one item in liters. The carrier bills bulky parcels by volume: the billable weight in pounds is the larger of the actual weight in pounds and the dimensional weight, which is the parcel's volume in cubic inches divided by 139. A parcel's volume is `qty × volume_l`. One inch is exactly 2.54 cm, and one liter is exactly 1000 cm³. Products without `volume_l` are billed by actual weight as before. Pass the billable weight to `rate`.

Example input:

    {"orders": [{"id": "C1", "product": "pillow", "qty": 2}], "catalog": {"pillow": {"weight_kg": 0.5, "volume_l": 20}}, "usd_to_sek": 10}

Example output:

    {"quotes_sek": {"C1": 198.0}, "total_sek": 198.0, "unknown": [], "matched": {}}
