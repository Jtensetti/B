# Step 3: Swedish kronor

Keep everything from before, but customers now pay in Swedish kronor. The input has a new key `"usd_to_sek": <number>`, the number of kronor per US dollar. Replace `quotes` by `quotes_sek` and `total_usd` by `total_sek`. Each quote in kronor is `round(usd × usd_to_sek, 2)`. `total_sek` is `round(sum of the kronor quotes, 2)`. `unknown` and `matched` stay as they are.

Example input:

    {"orders": [{"id": "A1", "product": "kettle", "qty": 2}], "catalog": {"kettle": {"weight_kg": 1.2}}, "usd_to_sek": 10.5}

Example output:

    {"quotes_sek": {"A1": 100.8}, "total_sek": 100.8, "unknown": [], "matched": {}}
