# Step 5: express

Keep everything from before. An order may now have `"express": true`. Quote such an order with `rate(billable_weight_lb, True)`. Orders without the key, or with `false`, are quoted as before.

Example input:

    {"orders": [{"id": "D1", "product": "kettle", "qty": 1, "express": true}, {"id": "D2", "product": "kettle", "qty": 1}], "catalog": {"kettle": {"weight_kg": 1.2}}, "usd_to_sek": 10}

Example output:

    {"quotes_sek": {"D1": 112.8, "D2": 70.5}, "total_sek": 183.3, "unknown": [], "matched": {}}
